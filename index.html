Jeepney Dash

Pick up passengers, dodge traffic, and keep the route going.

How to play

  • ⬅️ ➡️ or A / D: change lane
  • 📱 Tap the left or right side of the screen
  • 🧍 Pick up passengers: +10
  • ⛽ Grab fuel: +25 and a heart
  • 🚗 🚧 🕳️ Crashes cost a heart
  • Lose all 3 hearts and the trip ends
